# Sprint 5 pricing and quote contracts

Sprint 5 adds exact SAR pricing catalogs and a quote aggregate without implementing payments. Money is stored in signed PostgreSQL `bigint` halalas (`*_minor`), accepted and serialized by APIs as canonical non-negative decimal strings. Calculations use bounded `BigInt` arithmetic and positive half-up integer rounding; JavaScript floating-point arithmetic is never used for money. Provider prices explicitly declare whether VAT is included and a VAT rate in basis points. QaaAt does not claim tax-principal ownership, commission, or provider-net policy.

Pricing modes are `hourly`, `fixed_session`, `half_day`, `full_day`, `package`, and `custom_quote`. Mode-specific fields are rejected when irrelevant. Rate plans, service options, and packages are tenant-scoped, activatable, archivable, and snapshotted into quote line items. Existing Hall `services` remain legacy descriptive values; they are not migrated into priced `service_options` automatically.

Quotes use `draft → sent → accepted | customer_declined | expired | withdrawn`. Revision persistence allows only `draft → sent → superseded`; sent and superseded snapshots and line items are database-immutable. Editing after send creates a new draft revision; sending it supersedes the prior sent revision without modifying its line items. The server resolves selected catalog records and calculates subtotal, discount, VAT, deposit representation, remaining amount, and total. Rounding is deterministic to the nearest halala using positive half-up integer arithmetic.

`quote_revisions.prices_include_vat` means every line total in that revision follows the same display policy. Each line snapshots its resolved source policy in `quote_line_items.prices_include_vat`. Mixed inclusive/exclusive sources are rejected with `QUOTE_TAX_POLICY_MIXED`; the revision field can therefore never contradict its lines.

Draft and sent quotes never block inventory. Customer acceptance locks the Quote and sent revision, verifies expiry/ownership/current revision/company/Space, revalidates Sprint 3 availability, creates one Space-backed Booking, then creates the existing payment-purpose hold and inventory block in the same transaction. The Booking becomes `accepted` awaiting payment; neither Quote nor Booking is marked paid or confirmed. Hold duration is category-configured with an optional Space override, bounded to 1–72 hours and defaulting to 48 hours, then follows the Sprint 3 `payment_expired` cleanup path.

Accepted Bookings snapshot `accepted_quote_id`, `accepted_quote_revision_id`, and exact `accepted_total_minor` under database foreign keys. Booking APIs return exact `totalPriceDecimal` and quote-backed `totalPriceMinor` strings without Quote lookups. The legacy numeric `totalPrice` is returned only when the amount's minor units do not exceed `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`; larger values return `null`, preventing rounded compatibility output. These Booking-row snapshots also keep paginated lists free of per-row Quote queries.

Company endpoints require `pricing.view/manage` or `quotes.view/manage`. Customer reads expose only sent historical revisions and omit provider internal notes. Quote events and notification intents are durable and transactionally written. Expiry workers use bounded `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` processing.

Rollback is refused after an accepted Quote references a Booking, preserving commercial history. No Sprint 6 payment provider, attempt, webhook, refund, invoice, payout, commission, advertisement, review, dynamic pricing, or instant-booking behavior is included.
