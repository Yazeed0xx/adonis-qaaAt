# Sprint 3 availability and inventory decisions

- Schedule configuration and inventory consumption are separate. Weekly hours, sessions, and `availability_exceptions` generate or remove candidates and never create inventory blocks. A `closed` exception is configuration. An `external_reservations.type = closure` record is an owned, audited operational block.
- `space_inventory_blocks` is the only overlap authority. Every row references exactly one Booking, Booking hold, or external reservation; partial unique indexes prevent duplicate source blocks. PostgreSQL `btree_gist` and the exclusion constraint are mandatory production prerequisites.
- Pending requests may overlap and do not block. Booking acceptance atomically creates an expiring payment-purpose hold and block before changing the Booking to `accepted`. Overlap leaves it pending. Hold expiry changes it to `payment_expired`; cancellation releases its hold and block.
- One authoritative policy assertion runs both when a request is created and again inside the acceptance transaction. It enforces the effective weekly/exception schedule, availability mode, requested duration, minimum notice, advance horizon, and exact named-session or contiguous range contract before any hold or block is written.
- `hourly` accepts a requested duration between the configured minimum and maximum. `session` offers the configured localized named sessions and requires an exact session range. `full_day` offers one complete configured daily window. `multi_day` validates and offers one contiguous range across the requested configured days; preparation and cleanup buffers are applied by the inventory block ledger.
- Company calendar management exposes read/write policy and session contracts plus list/create/update/delete exception contracts. Wall times are strict, `closed` has no times, `modified_hours` and `open_override` require times, and overlapping operating/session/exception windows are rejected.
- Existing Saudi Venues default to `Asia/Riyadh`. Concrete records store UTC instants plus original local strings and IANA zone. Inputs require explicit offsets. Venue timezone changes are rejected when future calendar data exists.
- Public availability is limited to 31 days and 5,000 generated slots. Company feeds are limited to 93 days and 100 rows per page. Rules, exceptions, and blocks are loaded in batches, never queried once per slot.
- The legacy Hall availability route keeps its envelope but projects the mapped Space policy and ledger. Pending requests no longer make slots unavailable.
- `external_hold` always expires. Cancellation and expiry release blocks without deleting history. The bounded cleanup job processes Booking holds and external holds; audit and notification intent are committed with state changes.
- External-hold expiry claims a bounded batch with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, so concurrent workers count and process each hold once. New and updated external holds must expire in the future.
- The migration writes a status-count report before backfill. Future confirmed bookings become blocks. Future accepted bookings with future deadlines become holds/blocks. Accepted bookings with missing or elapsed deadlines become `payment_expired`. Pending and historical terminal bookings do not create active blocks.
- Rollback deterministically maps `payment_expired` to the legacy-supported `expired` state before restoring the old Booking status constraint.
- Pricing, quotes, visits, PSP/fake payments, and Sprint 4 are excluded.
